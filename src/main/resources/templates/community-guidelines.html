CASEZERO COMMUNITY STANDARD

Community Guidelines

CASEZERO exists to support responsible public awareness and research about unresolved cases in Pakistan. These rules protect the dignity, privacy and safety of people connected to every case.

Read-only policy page: members cannot edit or submit changes to these guidelines.

1. Use reliable public sources

  • Share information that is already lawfully available to the public.
  • Include a working source link and identify the publisher where possible.
  • Clearly distinguish confirmed reporting, allegations and unverified research leads.

2. Protect privacy and dignity

  • Never publish private addresses, phone numbers, identity documents or private account details.
  • Do not expose private information about victims, families, witnesses or other individuals.
  • Avoid graphic, exploitative or disrespectful material.

3. No accusations or harassment

  • Do not identify or accuse a person as responsible for a crime without reliable, attributable public reporting.
  • No threats, hate speech, bullying, harassment or calls for retaliation.
  • Do not encourage users to confront, follow or contact people connected to a case.

4. Keep research responsible

  • Do not trespass, hack accounts, impersonate officials or obtain information unlawfully.
  • Do not interfere with police work, legal proceedings or an active investigation.
  • Do not present speculation as fact or alter evidence and source material.

5. Contribute constructively

  • Keep comments relevant, respectful and focused on documented information.
  • Explain corrections with sources instead of arguments or personal attacks.
  • Respect moderation decisions and use Contact Us to report a concern.

6. Moderation and review

  • Submissions may be reviewed before appearing publicly.
  • Content that violates these rules may be hidden or removed by administrators.
  • Repeated or serious violations may result in account restrictions.
Important: CASEZERO is an independent public-information and educational project. It is not an emergency service, police authority or substitute for legal advice. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency services.
